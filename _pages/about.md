Hi! I'm Hanbin Zheng, an undergraduate in the dual-degree program of the [Zhejiang University – University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign Institute](https://zjui.intl.zju.edu.cn/en) (ZJUI). I study **Computer Engineering** (UIUC) and **Electronic and Computer Engineering** (ZJU), and I expect to graduate in 2028.

My interests lie in **generative modeling** and **decision making**. I worked as an undergraduate researcher with [Prof. Xiangming Meng](https://zjui.intl.zju.edu.cn/en/team/teacherinfo/2082), studying flow-based generative models, including flow matching, MeanFlow, and Gaussian-mixture flows, and using them for image inverse problems. Recently, I have been teaching myself deep reinforcement learning by working through UC Berkeley's CS285.

I also like building things that move. On my university's RoboMaster team, I developed the electrical control system for a hero robot, including the quaternion-based orientation estimation running on its STM32 controller.

I enjoy mathematics, and I write [notes on this blog](/blog/) about it and about whatever else I'm learning. Outside of work, I'm an amateur long-distance runner.

_Undergraduate research at ZJUI, advised by [Prof. Xiangming Meng](https://zjui.intl.zju.edu.cn/en/team/teacherinfo/2082), Jun 2025 – Dec 2025._

In this project I studied **Variational Flow Matching** and **Gaussian Mixture Flow Matching (GMFlow)** for image inverse problems. GMFlow does not predict a single velocity. Instead, it models the velocity distribution as a Gaussian mixture, which can represent multi-modal posteriors. I implemented 2D toy examples to test our new algorithm before scaling it to images.

**Code:** [github.com/hanbinzheng/naive-gmflow](https://github.com/hanbinzheng/naive-gmflow)

_Undergraduate research at ZJUI, advised by [Prof. Xiangming Meng](https://zjui.intl.zju.edu.cn/en/team/teacherinfo/2082), Jun 2025 – Dec 2025._

Flow matching trains a neural network to predict a time-dependent velocity field $$u_t(x)$$. Integrating this field moves samples from a simple source distribution, usually a standard Gaussian, to the data distribution. In this project I studied the framework in depth and implemented conditional image generation on **MNIST** and **CIFAR-10** from scratch.

The density path $$p_t$$ generated by the velocity field satisfies the [continuity equation](/blog/2025/the-continuity-equation/):

$$
\frac{\partial p_t(x)}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot \big(p_t(x)\, u_t(x)\big) = 0 .
$$

_Self-study, Jul 2026 – present._

I am working through UC Berkeley's [CS285: Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse/) and implementing its core algorithms from scratch:

- **Behavioral cloning** with a flow-matching policy
- **Policy gradients**
- **Actor-critic** methods
- **Deep Q-learning**

I trained and benchmarked the agents on environments such as **PushT**, **InvertedPendulum**, and **LunarLander**.
